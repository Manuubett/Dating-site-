At Bett Hub, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website https://bett.website and use our services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, and profile photo (optional).
  • Content You Post: Public posts, comments, and profile information are visible to all users and search engines.
  • Usage Data: We collect anonymized data about how you interact with the site (e.g., pages visited, time spent).
  • Cookies: We use essential cookies for login sessions and analytics (via Google Analytics, privacy-compliant).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide and improve our social platform
  • Send you email notifications (you can opt out)
  • Prevent spam, fraud, and abuse
  • Display relevant content and ads (after AdSense approval)

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share:

  • With service providers (Firebase, Google Analytics) under strict confidentiality
  • If required by law
  • With your consent

Important: We never sell your personal information to third parties. Your data privacy is our priority.

4. Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your account data
  • Opt out of email communications
  • Request data export (via Contact Us)

5. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS, Firebase Security Rules) to protect your data. However, no system is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Bett Hub is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with the updated date.

8. Contact Us

Questions? Email us at: betthub11@gmail.com

We typically respond to privacy-related inquiries within 2 business days.
